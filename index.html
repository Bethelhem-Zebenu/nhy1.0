About Us

NHY is one of Ethiopia’s leading import-focused companies, delivering trusted products across key industries.

Mission

To serve as Ethiopia's premier gateway for authentic global brands by anchoring secure supply chains, eradicating counterfeit goods, and delivering world-class retail experiences.

Vision

To position Ethiopia as a leading luxury travel retail hub across East Africa through enterprise expansion, strategic global partnerships, and keep an unwavering service excellence.

Core Values

Driven by integrity, quality, passion, and determination to elevate local standards, protect the domestic consumer market, and sustain community-focused growth everywhere.
